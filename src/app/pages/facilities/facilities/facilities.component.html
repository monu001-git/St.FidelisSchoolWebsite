Facilities

St. Fidelis School, Kheda Khush Khabar, Aligarh

St. Fidelis School, Aligarh, stands out as one of the finest institutions dedicated to fostering holistic growth in education and personality development. The school is equipped with state-of-the-art facilities, including modern teaching aids like electronic smart boards, enhancing the teaching-learning experience.

To ensure all-round development, the curriculum integrates a variety of engaging activities such as fancy dress, recitation, show-and-tell, cooking without fire, and pot decoration. These activities provide students with opportunities to explore their creativity, build confidence, and grow in multiple dimensions.

Computer Lab

Preparing Students for a Digital World: The St. Fidelis Computer Lab

At St. Fidelis School, we understand the essential role of computers in modern life. Our computer lab provides students with the tools and skills they need to thrive in this digital age. Students gain access to a world of information, enhance their learning through interactive software, and develop essential computer skills for future success. We are committed to equipping our students for the challenges and opportunities of the 21st century.

Library

Fostering a Love for Reading: The St. Fidelis School Library

Our library is a vibrant space where students can explore a diverse collection of resources, enhance their English skills, and develop a lifelong love for reading. It's a valuable resource center for both students and teachers, supporting learning and fostering information literacy.

Kids Corner

Playtime at St. Fidelis: Essential for Growth

Our spacious playground, complete with a dedicated Kids Corner and swings, provides a fun and vital space for students to engage in physical activity, boost their health, develop social skills, and unleash their creativity. Playtime is an integral part of the holistic learning experience at St. Fidelis School, Aligarh.

Maths Lab

The St. Fidelis Maths Lab: Making Maths Fun

Our Maths Lab provides a stimulating environment where students can engage with mathematical concepts through hands-on activities, games, and technology. This interactive approach fosters conceptual understanding, problem-solving skills, and a positive attitude towards maths.

Class Rooms

St. Fidelis Classrooms: Bright Spaces for Learning

Our classrooms are designed for comfort and learning, with features like ergonomic furniture, ample natural light, and interactive whiteboards. These vibrant spaces create a positive and engaging environment where students can thrive.
