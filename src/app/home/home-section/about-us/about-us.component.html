St. Fidelis School is founded and administered by the Catholic Archdiocese of Agra. It is a Catholic School, a minority Institution under Article 30 of the Indian Constitution.

The school aims at imparting value based education to the children of the citizens of Aligarh without any distinction of caste, creed, social status or sex and enhance all round development of the students.
